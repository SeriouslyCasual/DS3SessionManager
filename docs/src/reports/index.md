# Progress reports

Formal progress reports for the project, newest first.

## Progress Report 1, November 13, 2026

**[Download the PDF](progress-report-1.pdf)** (3 pages)

- The **literature review**, the **Steam peer-to-peer architecture study** and the **examination of existing tools** are complete. The key takeaways are summarised on the [research summary](../research/summary.md) page.
- The measurement approach is set. Steam's own connectivity checks are timed at the operating-system level to measure round-trip time, jitter and loss per peer. Each connection is compared with the speed-of-light limit for its distance.
- The experiment plan covers measurement accuracy, direct vs. relayed paths, geographic latency inflation, and alternative routes. See the [research roadmap](../roadmap.md).
